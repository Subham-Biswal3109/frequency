Regulatory basis:
NFAP-2025 covers frequency allocation up to 3000 GHz. It states that NFAP
does not itself provide the right to use spectrum and that a frequency
assignment is required from WPC unless exempted.

Dataset note:
The 25-band reference table is regulatory/reference information derived
from the earlier NFAP-2025 starter dataset. The large observation and
candidate files are synthetic and clearly labelled as such.
